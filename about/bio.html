Bio

Daniel Sinclair (NZ)(TW) is a designer based in London, recently graduated from Goldsmiths, University of London. His practice explores our relationship with non-human and current technological worlds, using design to imagine alternative optimisations to the systems that shape our lives. His work challenges efficiency as the only standard, pursuing poetic and speculative explorations. Technologies that present themselves as thoughtfully designed often leave little room for the user’s agency and imagination. For him, this calls for new vocabularies of interaction and a mindfulness towards how the environments we shape, in turn, shape us. He grounds this inquiry through making, moving between physical interaction, hardware, and digital interfaces as both material and dialogue.
