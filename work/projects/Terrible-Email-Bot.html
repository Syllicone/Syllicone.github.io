Terrible Email Bot

2026

Sending generated emails might boost efficiency and bring instant gratification, but for organising coffee chats, asking for portfolio feedback, and studio applications, quantity isn't your friend. Generic emails don't sound like you.

Terrible Email bot has a 'bad character' to write to a studio, schedule it to send in a week, and countdown the hours you have to edit it before it goes out. Copy and paste garbles up the text to prevent any external writing.

When the barrier to reaching out is a blank 'new message' space, this tool creates the deadline, the structure, and the motivation (terror).
